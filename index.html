Policy diffusion

Reporting regulation, stock liquidity and international adoption.

Sample and data

Market dataDatastream, 2000–2025. 90 jurisdictions, 1,055,142 stock-year observations and 99,100 company records. One stock-year is one stock observed for one year.
Policy data17 regulation families. National implementation dates, policy histories, legal scope and source documents. Public disclosure, confidential tax reporting and regulatory institutions remain distinct.
International comparisons15 families in the first year; nine over the longer window. National dates are linked to the global market panel.
Diffusion dataEarlier adoption among trade and institutional peers, neighbouring economic outcomes, IMF credit and aid.

First-year treated exposure: 30,703 companies in 48 jurisdictions. Longer-window treated exposure: 18,470 companies in 36 jurisdictions. Companies can appear under several policies.

We compare stock-market changes around national implementation with changes in comparison jurisdictions. This measures exposure to a policy environment; it does not mean every listed company has the same reporting obligation.

Design and scope

Each family has separate national implementation cohorts. Comparisons exclude countries already implementing that family; missing histories are not treated as nonadoption. The bank geographic contrast also includes explicitly coded non-EU jurisdictions, which can have affected subsidiaries or other national rules.

One largest pre-implementation share line per company is held fixed within a cohort. GDP is divided across complete companies within each country. Family weights are equal or proportional to sampled pre-implementation market value; companies and policy exposures can recur. Thirty complete companies per country and three comparison countries are required.

Dates identify documented policy phases, rather than a universal first-ever reporting duty. E-only climate rules are separate from broader nonfinancial reporting. Confidential tax country reports are not investor disclosures. Group histories, thresholds and foreign-listing exemptions are not fully observed.
