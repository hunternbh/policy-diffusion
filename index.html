Sample and datasets

Market data cover 90 countries, 2000–2025. The public-disclosure calendar records 269 country–family topic milestones in 66 countries, across ten reporting families. The main liquidity comparison uses 64 implementations, 47 countries and 6 families.

1. Liquidity

The equal-family stacked estimate is 10.2% lower trading costs (p = 0.268). The market-cap family average is 17.1% lower trading costs (p = 0.043). Lower trading costs indicate greater stock liquidity.

EU climate reporting

The full climate comparison includes 14 adopting countries: Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Spain, Sweden, the UK and Vietnam. The estimated trading-cost change is -18.0% (p = 0.086); the 95% interval in logs is [-0.429, 0.031].

Countries and sources

The calendar matches all 38 Krueger treatment countries. It records all 32 Rauter jurisdictions; 31 are in the market-data universe. First covered fiscal periods are distinguished from legal entry into force. The IFRS reconciliation includes Norway, the Philippines and Singapore’s later IFRS-identical framework.

Switzerland’s alternative-GAAP scope and Venezuela’s conflicting historical IFRS timing remain explicit exclusions. Published lists are historical benchmarks; they do not certify every issuer’s eligibility or actual filing date. Source documents and retrieval outcomes are preserved locally.
