Disclosure regulation paper: next steps

Research working draft; not submission-ready

Public-disclosure spread point estimates are negative under both family weights and horizons. The primary estimates and diagnostics do not establish a general causal liquidity improvement. Diffusion associations are conditional and sensitive to common family waves.

1. Complete the historical disclosure universe
The 482 recorded country-family histories identify documented phases, not a verified census of first mandatory disclosure and nonadoption. Unknown histories narrow the comparison sample.
Output: Country-family-year calendar with original legal sources, earliest mandatory/public dates, subsequent phases, repeal and verified nonadoption through the observation window. Separate E, S and G obligations and publication audiences.
Completion: Every treated and comparison country-year in the proposed main sample has a reviewed historical status and a source-supported date. Unresolved histories remain missing, never assumed untreated.

2. Identify when investors could first receive the information
Announcement, first covered financial period, statutory filing deadline and actual publication are different events. The global calendar currently has no company-level actual filing dates.
Output: Separate legal announcement, application, reporting-period, deadline and public-release clocks; verify transitions against issuer reports and registry records where available.
Completion: The main information clock is chosen for its institutional meaning before refitting; alternative clocks and coverage attrition are reported on comparable samples.

3. Strengthen liquidity identification
Fixed cohorts and not-yet-treated comparisons do not resolve policy endogeneity, coordinated adoption waves, overlapping reforms or pre-policy differences. Matching retains material imbalance.
Output: An institutional identification memo, overlap and coordinated-wave map, credible comparison sample, and an estimator appropriate to the verified staggered timing. If a narrow eligibility threshold or externally imposed implementation shock exists, document it rather than assume one.
Completion: A defensible counterfactual and clustered inference match the legal assignment mechanism. Event dynamics, balance, trend-sensitivity restrictions and alternative outcomes are evaluated without selecting by sign.

4. Extend mechanism tests on adequate global support
The current spread interactions have only two supported public families. A country-level regulator-quality proxy is not firm-level enforcement or information demand.
Output: Pre-specified mechanism predictions with distinct alternatives, global coverage audit and source-linked moderators; issuer eligibility and historical fundamentals if issuer-level tests are used.
Completion: Cross-sectional comparisons cover enough independently adopting jurisdictions and families for the claimed population, with formal interaction tests and multiplicity disclosure. No numerical support target is invented as a guarantee of identification.

5. Complete diffusion histories and separate common waves
The public-sample reference joint logit has no usable rows. The positive common-year language association changes sign with family-year effects. Observed phase histories are retrospectively selected.
Output: Complete adoption-risk sets, documented peer universes, coordinated-legal-group exclusions and separately justified learning, competition and emulation measures.
Completion: Separate and joint tests retain interpretable outcome variation; conclusions survive relevant common-wave explanations or are explicitly limited to conditional associations.

6. Finish independent scholarly review and replication
The literature matrix is a targeted 34-record review, not a systematic full-text review of all cited papers. The public bundle cannot redistribute licensed Datastream inputs.
Output: Coauthor review of novelty and institutions; complete bibliography and source audit; independent reproduction using documented licensed-input locations and aggregate replication files.
Completion: Every central claim maps to a source or table, all exclusions and exploratory choices are disclosed, and an independent reader can reproduce the reported sample and estimates with authorised data access.
