# Policy diffusion

Global country-level reporting regulation, liquidity and policy adoption: https://hunternbh.github.io/policy-diffusion/

The main paper studies liquidity first, diffusion second. Seventeen policy families are considered; thirteen support following-year comparisons and ten support years 1-3. Prior-year GDP weights are used within families; both equal and sampled pre-implementation market-cap weights are retained across families. These are country-level exposures, not a census of legally covered companies.

`data/design-audit/` contains the complete 1,296-cell retrospective liquidity grid, reference-paper benchmarks, date audit, paired weights, 104 diffusion coefficient attempts, unsupported fits, source manifest and method comparison. All signs are retained. The main findings remain imprecise and dependent on design; selected significant models do not replace the declared principal tests.

`downloads/international-policy-results.zip` includes aggregate data, code and the analysis plan. Licensed company observations and identifiers remain local. `data/international/` preserves the preceding country analysis; selected company evidence and legal source records remain linked in the appendix.
