# Disclosure regulation, liquidity and policy diffusion

[Read the report](https://hunternbh.github.io/policy-diffusion/) · [Paper](downloads/paper.pdf) · [Aggregate tables and code](downloads/disclosure-paper-results.zip)

Global country-level analysis of identified disclosure regulations. Public requirements form the primary pool; confidential reporting and broader regulation categories remain separate sensitivities. Both equal-family and pre-policy market-cap family weights are reported.

The working paper has 15 tables, including 12 with estimated regression results, and three figures. Historical treatment gaps, policy identification and limited joint diffusion support remain research priorities. The report does not assert a general causal liquidity benefit or submission readiness. See [next steps](data/disclosure-paper/submission-readiness.txt).

Licensed market inputs and original third-party papers are not redistributed. All reported estimates, unsupported fits and analysis choices are retained.
