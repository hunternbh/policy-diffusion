# Reporting regulation, issuer liquidity, and diffusion

Public research report: https://hunternbh.github.io/policy-diffusion/

The principal paper reports individual issuer outcomes under geographic policy exposure across 17 attempted families, alongside source-corroborated studies of auditor reporting, XBRL, conflict minerals, banking, IFRS, statutory nonfinancial and extractive reporting. Both equal and pre-event market-cap family weights are reported. The 8-page paper has 3 tables and 2 figures.

Source-supported issuer studies cover seven selected first-year families and five balanced families, not all 17 global eligible-issuer populations. Missing thresholds, legal perimeters, first histories, and confidential tax dates are explicitly dispositioned. No unsupported family is assigned zero.

`data/issuers/` contains all aggregate results, support failures, weights, and audit summaries; `downloads/issuer-regulation-findings.zip` also contains 441 additive event-scope JSONs. Original source-linked regulation records, prior source reviews and country-market results remain preserved. Licensed market/financial observations are not distributed.

Static GitHub Pages site; serve the repository root over HTTP to use the interactive tables.
